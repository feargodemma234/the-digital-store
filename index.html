Important payment information

The product price is displayed in USD. Your payment must be made using the selected cryptocurrency.

Use the correct wallet address and the correct network shown above.

Do not send a different cryptocurrency or use a different network. Sending funds to the wrong address or using the wrong network may result in permanent loss of funds.

Payment instructions

  1. Select the cryptocurrency you want to use.
  2. Check that the network is correct.
  3. Copy the QKJ Store wallet address.
  4. Send the required payment from your crypto wallet.
  5. Wait for the transaction to be broadcast to the blockchain.
  6. Copy your transaction hash/ID.
  7. Paste the transaction hash into the verification box below.
  8. Click Verify Payment .
Enter the blockchain transaction hash/ID for the payment you sent. Do not enter your wallet address.

🔒 Secure verification: QKJ Store checks the transaction against blockchain data before providing the product download.

A transaction hash by itself does not prove that a payment was made.
